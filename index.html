Questions, answered

Everything you're wondering.

Straight answers about working with a full-service digital marketing agency.

What does a digital marketing agency actually do?+

A full-service digital marketing agency like NAQQAR handles every channel that grows your brand online — social media management, SEO, content writing, graphic design, paid ad running, and eCommerce management — so you're not juggling five freelancers with five different strategies.

How much does social media management cost?+

It depends on scope — number of platforms, posting frequency, and whether paid ads are included. Tell us your goals in the service request form and we'll come back with a plan and a real number, not a guess.

Can you manage our Amazon and Etsy stores together?+

Yes — eCommerce management across Amazon, Etsy, and your own website is one of our core specialties, run as one coordinated storefront strategy instead of three disconnected accounts.

How long until we see SEO results?+

Most brands see meaningful ranking movement within 60–90 days, with stronger compounding results after month four as content, links, and technical fixes stack together.

Do you offer training for our in-house team?+

Yes — business development and training is a core service, covering everything from marketing strategy to hands-on tool training for platforms like Meta Business Suite and Amazon Seller Central.
